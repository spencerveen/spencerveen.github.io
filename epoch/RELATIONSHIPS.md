# EPOCH — Phase 7A.1: Friendships and Partnerships

**Status:** First playable social relationship foundation.

## Why this phase exists
Settlers previously had factual clanmate and housemate connections, but
they did not remember friendships or form partnerships. Relationships now
persist across saves and contribute to the settlement's personal history.

## Real social contact, not arbitrary yearly matchmaking
- The game clock calls the social system once per active second. No affinity
  is generated by render frames, idle workers or loaded saves in the background.
- Two residents must both be visibly **socializing** and within **5.5 m**.
  Work, sleep, exhaustion, hunger and food trips continue to override leisure.
- Each **six accumulated seconds** of valid shared conversation grants that
  pair **one affinity point**, up to 100. Reversing or duplicating a pair
  cannot double progress on a clock tick.
- Sharing a real constructed **Hut** grants a small **+1 affinity per year**
  to each pair of residents living there. The generic communal camp never
  grants that housing bonus. Housing does not change automatically.
- **Friendship** begins at 18 affinity; **close friendship** at 38.
- A **partnership** can begin at 48 affinity if both villagers are at least
  18, have earned 32 conversation points and neither already has a partner.
  Partnerships are reciprocal and exclusive in this first iteration.
- Each first friendship and partnership adds a historical note to both
  villagers' personal chronicles; the event appears in settlement history.
- No children, marriage ceremonies, forced pairing, romance buttons, sexual
  mechanics, spouse relocation or invented family ties are introduced.

## Saving, dying, and becoming the leader
Relationships are stored once per pair in `village.relationships`, under
a stable key formed by the two saved resident IDs. Loading sanitizes the
records, rejects nonexistent villagers, and prevents duplicate partners.

When a resident dies, living partners gain a mourning memory. Formerly
deceased individuals are no longer available for new interactions. When
an existing villager succeeds the founder as player leader, their previous
relationships remain saved, and other residents recognize that partner as
the active village leader. The player cannot currently earn new NPC social
points directly. That requires later character interaction gameplay.

## Where to view it
**Council Lodge → Overview** shows friendship/partnership counts. **People
& Jobs** displays per-resident social summaries. Click an individual
resident to see their affinities, friendships, partners, shared Hut years,
and personal chronicle; other resident names link to their profiles.

## Next steps in Phase 7
**7A.2** introduces family formation and children using established
relationships and real housing/food constraints. **7A.3** adds childhood
and adulthood. **7A.4** connects family trees and inheritance to
succession. None of these later mechanics are claimed as complete here.
